# Lume V3.8 — Super Admin Dashboard

## Added
- Business dashboard.
- Revenue total.
- Paid/pending order counts.
- Revenue chart by day.
- Sales split: member vs premium.
- Active membership count.
- Top premium content by revenue.
- Creator performance ranking.
- CSV transaction export.
- Date range selector: 7 / 30 / 90 / 365 days.

## Visibility
All financial information remains under `/admin` and is not exposed through public creator APIs.

## Accounting note
The dashboard reports gross transaction amounts recorded as paid. It does not yet model payment-provider fees, refunds, taxes, creator commission, or net profit. Those should be added only when the business rules are finalized.
